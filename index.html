Key SA insights
BRCSA confirmed 12.5% drop in linear TV households (2019–2023) as audiences migrated to streaming. When streaming is included, 91% of HH still consume TV content — the screen hasn't died, the pipe has changed. (BRCSA 2023)
Streaming accounts for 28% of all video viewing in SA (BRCSA 2023). CTV ad impressions on YouTube grew +200% YoY since 2022 (Google SA / RetailingAfrica 2023).
DStv lost 7% of ad revenue in FY2023 directly attributable to competitive pressure from digital channels. SABC ad revenue fell R600M in the same year. (TechCabal 2024)
PwC projects CTV ad revenue to grow from R237M (2023) to R682M by 2028 — a 188% increase. Digital advertising will account for 67% of total SA ad spend by 2028. (PwC E&M Outlook 2024)
Gen Z and Millennials make up 83% of streaming viewers. 51% live in metros. 76% would accept ads for a lower-cost streaming plan. (BB Media 2024 / MAPS Survey 2023)

Interpretation note
Daily linear TV viewers represents people. Connected TV households represents devices. Applying a 2.7x viewers-per-household multiplier (aligned with SA household size and shared viewing behaviour) gives a like-for-like comparison. By 2025 est., CTV daily viewers (~32M) substantially exceed linear daily viewers (~7.9M). EST
